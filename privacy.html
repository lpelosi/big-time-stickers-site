Privacy Policy

Big Time Stickers · Effective 3 November 2026

Big Time Stickers does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Full stop.

What the app does with your data

  • Stickers you add (from iMessage, Photos, Files, the clipboard, or the share sheet) are stored only on your device, in the app's private container that is shared with its iMessage and share extensions.
  • Search words are produced by Apple's on-device image recognition and stored alongside the sticker on your device. No image ever leaves the device for analysis.
  • Usage counts (which stickers you send most, and most recently) are kept on the device to order the Frequent and Recent tabs.

What the app does not do

  • No network access. The app makes no connections to any server.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your Photo Library beyond the pictures you pick with the system picker. No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.
  • No reading of your messages. The iMessage extension only receives stickers you choose to send and stickers you tap on the sticker keyboard while importing.

Deleting your data

Delete stickers inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
