Big Time Stickers

Pin, sort and resend your stickers. Everything stays on your phone.

How it works

  • Bring over iMessage stickers. Open Import from iMessage, tap a sticker on the sticker keyboard (or drag it onto the landing pad). It lands in the app.
  • Add from anywhere. Photos, Files, the clipboard, or the share sheet from any app. Lift the subject out of a photo to make a sticker.
  • Effects. Outline, Rainbow, Comic, Puffy, Glitter, rendered on the device.
  • Folders and tabs. Sort into folders, put the iMessage keyboard tabs in any order, pin your go-tos.
  • Search. The app recognises what is in each sticker on the device, so "dog" or "cake" finds it without typing tags.
  • In Messages. Messages → + → More → Big Time Stickers. Send, pin, unpin and import without leaving the conversation.

Frequently asked

Can it import my whole iMessage sticker drawer at once?
iOS offers no way for an app to read the drawer, so stickers come over one tap at a time. On a Mac with Messages in iCloud, the app's export script copies the drawer to iCloud Drive for a one-step folder import.

Does it need an account or internet?
No. There is no account, no server, and the app never connects to the network.

Where are my stickers stored?
In the app's own container on your device. Delete the app and they go with it, so keep originals you care about.
